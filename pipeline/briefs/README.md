# Pipeline briefs — Cowork workstream

Two documents are authored in Claude Cowork (Persona and Brief Researcher, Opus 5) and dropped
here. They are the ground truth the runtime pipeline scans against, so they are written before the
pipeline runs on 18 September.

## 1. `persona-dossier.md`

Tracewell: a fictional four-person observability start-up in Linz. Cover the founding team and
their roles, what the product does, stage and funding position, customers, and the decisions they
are actually facing in early 2026. The venture is fictional; everything around it is real.

Also record the team's answers across the five Jöhnk et al. readiness categories, since F3 reads
from them.

## 2. `scanning-brief.md`

What the Scout scans against:

- **Incumbents and competitors** — real, named, described in verifiable public fact only.
  Dynatrace is portrayed factually and neutrally; this is an outreach asset, not a pitch against
  them.
- **Technology shifts** — for example OpenTelemetry and the direction of open standards.
- **Regulation** — real EU regulation with dates.
- **Sources** — the specific publications, repositories, registers and feeds worth watching, with
  URLs.
- **Time window** — the period signals must fall in.

## 3. `replay-candidates.md`

Real signals from about early 2026 whose outcome by September 2026 is publicly documented and
dated. These feed the decision log's retrospective replay (F4). Each candidate needs both the
original signal and the dated outcome source. Invented outcomes are a project-ending defect.
